## Lights (LEDs): everything the lights mean (for the user manual; from the code, host only, to be checked on the FM-1)
The setting "key light" (GLO page) can turn the key lights off or change the glow; the rules below are for lights on.
Buttons: PLAY lights on every 4th step while playing (green; red while recording). REC lights steady when armed with the sequencer stopped, and blinks together with PLAY (every 4th step) while recording. HOME lights on the HOME and SONG pages (it blinks in song mode). EDIT, SEQ, ARP, FX, LFO (the mixer), GLO light for the page you are on. SEL lights while a list or a question is open. OCT+ lights when you are on a later 16-step page or in patterns 17-32 (not on the Tonic, where OCT+ / OCT- transpose). SAVE blinks on the GLO page when there is something unsaved.
Keys, step pages (EDIT, SEQ): white keys = the steps of the selected track that are on (bright); while playing the key of the step playing flips (on becomes off, off becomes on) so you see the moving light. Black keys 1-8: the selected track is bright (it blinks when it is muted); other tracks that have notes are dim; empty or muted tracks are dark. Keys you are holding are lit as well.
Keys, HOME and SONG pages: white keys = patterns: ones with notes dim, the playing one (on the SONG page the selected bar's one) bright, a cued one blinking. The black keys show the old parts (they go when 808 / 303 / break are removed).
Keys, MIX page: the black key of the track (or part) whose knobs you are on is bright (blinking when muted), the others dim, muted ones dark.
Beat lights (wip231, fainter in wip232, faintest in wip233): white keys 1, 5, 9, 13 glow FAINT (one scan frame in 12; the dim level is one in 4) on every page except PERFORM, HOME, SONG, a question screen and while FUNC is held on the Tonic (there the white keys show patterns); only with the key light setting on AND GLO > 4/4 LED ON (wip233, settings_t.beat_off, row under LIGHTS, default OFF since wip234, saved); a lit key (step on, playhead) stays bright. The level is FAINT_DIV in firmware/src/app/plat_fm1.c (12 since wip233; raise it for fainter, above about 10 it may flicker: go back to 10 or 8 if it does).
Keys while FUNC is held: see the row "Lights while FUNC is held" (it includes the fast blink of a slot just pasted into). The HOME and SONG pages' white keys only show the patterns now; on HOME they pick nothing.
