FM1 TONIC

Drum machine firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, inspired by Microtonic (a trademark of Sonic Charge) but with its own engine, not the same one. This is the same code the FM-1 runs, in your browser.

Turn your phone sideways for bigger controls, then press Switch on: the panel fills the screen.

Preset 9 = blank canvas. Turn PRESETS to 09 and press YES / UP: you get the sounds with no trigs at all, to try and build your own beat.

Mouse and touch

  • Drag a knob up or down, or use the scroll wheel over it.
  • Click or touch buttons and keys. Several fingers at once work on a touch screen.
  • To hold something down (to try a combination with the mouse): Shift-click (or right-click) a button or a key, click it again to let go. Black key 11 is the FUNC button.

Keyboard

  • Tab moves between the knobs, buttons and keys. On a knob, the arrow keys turn it (Page Up / Page Down faster). On a button or key, Space or Enter presses it for as long as you hold it.
  • While the FM-1 has focus, shortcuts play it: white keys 1–8 Q–I, 9–16 A–K; black keys 1–11 1–0, - (11 = FUNC); Space PLAY, Enter REC, Esc HOME, Z / X OCT− / OCT+, C SEL, V FX, B ENV, N LFO, M EDIT, , GLO, . SAVE, / SEQ, ↑ ↓ turn SELECT.
  • Computer Shift = hold down. Shift + any of those keys (or Shift+Space on a focused button) keeps it held until you press it again. Example: Shift+- holds FUNC, then press 9 (ACCENT) to copy the pattern, or / (SEQ) for song mode.

Saving

SAVE keeps your patterns in this browser, as the FM-1 keeps them in its memory. Factory reset forgets them. It is the firmware, not a recording: what you hear is FM1 Tonic running, at the FM-1's 44.1 kHz.

4. Presets and saving

PRESETS knob40 places: 1 to 8 factory, 9 to 40 yours. OCT+ loads it, OCT- keeps your sound. Refused while playing.
SAVESAVE HERE? OCT+ saves in the loaded place; ALGORITHM picks another place; OCT- goes to CHOOSE SLOT, then the name. HOME leaves.
AutosaveYour project saves by itself 4 seconds after you stop touching things. The compressor, reverb and delay are kept only by SAVE, inside the preset.
Power-onPERFORM starts clean: reverb 0 %, delay 0 %, ALL FX on.

5. Lights

PLAYLights on every 4th step (red while recording). The button of the page you are on is lit.
Black keysThe selected track bright, muted tracks blink slowly, tracks with notes dim.
White keys 1, 5, 9, 13Glow faintly on the beats when GLO > SETTINGS > 4/4 LED is ON (off by default; not on PERFORM).

Install FM1 Tonic Latest Version

Connect the FM-1 to the computer with a USB data cable, directly (no hub), and use Chrome or Edge. Do not unplug the cable while it writes: it takes a few minutes and the FM-1 restarts by itself.

The firmware update deletes your presets. Already using FM1 Tonic on your FM-1 and just updating? Press Back Up my Presets first. It saves them to a zip, and the Restore section below puts them back.

Checking the update file.

Back Up my Presets downloads one zip with every preset you saved in slots 9-40 (09_name.tnp, 10_name.tnp, and so on). Presets 1-8 are part of the firmware and are not included. Nothing on the FM-1 changes, and it can keep playing.

This is custom firmware, version 1.0. The author accepts no responsibility if your FM-1 is damaged or misbehaves because of this firmware. If an install fails and the FM-1 no longer starts, recovering it needs a Transporter. To go back to the official firmware you need M-VAVE's updater, M-UPGRADE, and the official FM-1 V15 firmware from m-vave.com/download.

Receive the project from your FM-1

  
Connect the FM-1 with FM1 Tonic running (USB data cable, Chrome or Edge) and press Receive. The page reads the whole
    project from the FM-1: the 8 voices' sounds, the patterns, the song and the knob motion. Then switch on here to play it,
    and use Download the 8 voices to get its WAV files. This replaces the project kept in this browser. The FM-1 can keep playing;
    changes on the FM-1 that are not saved yet are left out (stop it first to include them).

  

  
  






  
Export my presets as .mtpreset

  
Reads presets from the FM-1 (USB data cable, Chrome or Edge) and saves them as .mtpreset files, the format of the Patternarium presets.
    Export all downloads one zip with a file per saved slot (09_name.mtpreset, 10_name.mtpreset, and so on);
    Export one downloads the file of the slot you choose. Nothing on the FM-1 changes, and it can keep playing. Slots 1-8 are part of the firmware and cannot be read from the FM-1.

  
A .mtpreset has no place for what the Tonic added, so these are left out: ARP notes, late hits, per-track cuts and sends, compressor, reverb and delay,
    and the muted state. Drum names become “Drum n”. Only the 8 patterns the FM-1 holds are written. The sounds, tempo, swing and those patterns come across.

  

  

    
    
  

  
  






  
Restore my presets

  
Choose what to restore: the zip made by Back up my presets, or .mtpreset files (Patternarium downloads), one or many, or a whole
    folder of them. The page converts the .mtpreset files itself (the same way as the tool that made the factory presets) and shows what it will write
    into which slot. Each preset in the list has a slot menu (1-40): choose where it goes. A number 9-40 at the start of the file name
    (09_name.mtpreset) is chosen for you; "first empty" puts it into the first empty slot of the FM-1. Slots 1-8 are the factory presets and cannot be written yet. You confirm, then it sends them to the FM-1 (USB data cable, Chrome or Edge). Each slot it writes replaces what is in that slot now; other
    slots stay. Press STOP on the FM-1 first: it refuses while playing. A Patternarium preset keeps its 8 sounds, tempo, swing and the first 8 patterns.

  

  

  

  

  
  

  
Clear all slots

  
Empties all 32 user slots (9-40) on the FM-1, to start with a white canvas. It works only after Back up my presets
    has downloaded a zip on this page, so your presets are safe in a file first. Presets 1-8 stay.
